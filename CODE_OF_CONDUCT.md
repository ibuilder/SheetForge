# Code of conduct

## The standard

Be decent. Assume competence and good faith in the person you are replying to, including when you
think they are wrong.

Specifically, and without being exhaustive:

- Critique the work, not the person. "This loses the calibration on re-import" is useful;
  "did you even test this" is not.
- Take disagreement about technical decisions as normal, and settle it with evidence — a test, a
  measurement, a specification — rather than by volume or seniority.
- Accept that people are working in different languages, timezones and levels of experience, and
  that a construction professional filing a bug is not obliged to know how a hash chain works.
- Do not harass, demean or discriminate against anyone. Sexualised language and imagery, personal
  or political attacks, deliberate misgendering, and publishing others' private information are not
  acceptable here.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainers through
[GitHub's report form](https://github.com/ibuilder/SheetForge/security/advisories/new) or by
contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers will respond with what the situation warrants: a private word, a public correction, a
removed comment, or a ban. A maintainer who breaks these rules is held to them more strictly, not
less.

## Scope

Applies in this repository, its issues and pull requests, and anywhere someone is representing the
project.
